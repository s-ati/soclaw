The SOCLAW Framework

6 dimensions that
define a perfect match.

S

Skills

Technical and domain skill matching against job requirements with weighted must-have and nice-to-have criteria.

O

Ownership

Measures initiative-taking vs. dependence on guidance. Identifies candidates who drive outcomes independently.

C

Context

Cultural and organizational context signals — corporate, startup, regulated, cross-functional environment fit.

L

Location

Onsite, hybrid, or remote compatibility. Hard-stop detection for location policy mismatches.

A

Adaptability

How candidates respond to change and feedback. Evaluates resilience and flexibility in dynamic environments.

W

Work Style

Organizational discipline, communication proactivity, and structured work habits assessment.
